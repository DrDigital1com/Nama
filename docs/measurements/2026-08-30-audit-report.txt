Warning
Images missing alt text

146 of 344 images have no alt attribute. Alt text is the only way Google and screen-reader users can understand image content; without it you lose image-search traffic and risk accessibility complaints.

What to do

Write short, descriptive alt text for every meaningful image, and leave alt="" only for purely decorative images.

Evidence: https://nama-c.com/ (55), https://nama-c.com/faq/ (5), https://nama-c.com/blog/ (5), https://nama-c.com/shop/ (28), https://nama-c.com/sign-up/ (1), https://nama-c.com/about-us/ (6), https://nama-c.com/delivery/ (9), https://nama-c.com/solutions/ (16), https://nama-c.com/contact-us/ (5), https://nama-c.com/wishlist-2/ (5), https://nama-c.com/coming-soon/ (6), https://nama-c.com/order-tracking/ (5)

No structured data on the site

No JSON-LD structured data was found on any crawled page. Structured data tells Google who you are and what you offer, and enables rich results such as ratings, prices and opening hours.

Add baseline JSON-LD: Organization or LocalBusiness on the homepage, and Product/Service/Article on content pages.
